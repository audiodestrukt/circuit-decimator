Cone (macOS)

Copy the plugin(s) into your plugin folders, then rescan in your DAW:

  Cone.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  Cone.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/   (Logic, GarageBand, other AU hosts)

(~/Library is hidden in Finder: Go menu -> Go to Folder... and paste the path.)
Universal build: Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later.
